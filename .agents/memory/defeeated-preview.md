---
name: Defeeated preview
description: Scope and progression rules for the in-fight defeat-scene preview.
---

The Defeeated option previews scenes where an adversary currently active in the arena defeats the heroine. Closing the preview restores the fight exactly as it was, including HP, score, positions, and defeated-enemy progress.

**Why:** The user chose “Derrotas da heroína causadas pelas adversárias” and requested the mode as an on-screen option.

**How to apply:** Only list opponent types currently active in the arena. Treat previews as non-destructive. Roxy and Karateka voluntary scenes must retain their accept/decline step inside the preview.
